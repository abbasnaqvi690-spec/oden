About Oden

Daniyal Abbas started Oden in 2022 to help brands and people grow on social media without spending their lives managing it.

Most of our clients are good at what they do. What they don't have is the time to decide what to post, how to position themselves, what their audience needs to see, or how to stay consistent for months instead of weeks.

Oden takes that off their plate. We're a marketing agency that handles the work from strategy to execution: strategy, content, branding, websites, paid acquisition and outreach. You can hire us for one piece or hand us the whole thing.
